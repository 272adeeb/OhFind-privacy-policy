# OhFind Privacy Policy

OhFind ("we") operates a Pinterest integration that publishes image pins
to our own Pinterest business account.

**Data we access:** Through Pinterest's official API, the app can read our
account profile and boards, and create pins on our boards. We do not
collect, store, or share personal data of any Pinterest user.

**Content:** All pinned images are original AI-generated product visuals
created by OhFind. No third-party personal data is processed.

**Data sharing:** We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

**Contact:** For questions about this policy, contact us via Instagram
@ohfind.daily.
